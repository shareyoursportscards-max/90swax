1999 market notes

1999September 26, 2026

First data load for 1999. Prices use the last three weeks of comps only — this market is moving fast enough that a July sale no longer tells you anything. The headline is the Metal Universe Linchpins #6, which sold at $1,250 in a PSA 8 — the most expensive Thomas card of the year and one of the toughest inserts of the era. Ultra Diamond Producers #2 filled all three grades on its own: $151 in a PSA 8, $225 in a PSA 9 and $700 in a PSA 10.

1999September 26, 2026

Skybox Premium Soul of the Game #12 was the busiest card of the year with seven graded sales. PSA 9 copies ran from $150 in late July to $660 on September 16 after 30 bids — the price more than quadrupled in seven weeks. Its PSA 8 hit $300 on September 13. UD Choice Starquest Red #SQ17 also cleared big money at $582 in a PSA 10.

1999September 26, 2026

Chrome inserts are where the volume is. Topps Chrome Lords of the Diamond #LD4 filled every grade ($25 / $40 / $275), the Refractor version took a PSA 9 to $275 on August 30, and Power Brokers #PB7 plus its Refractor added four more filled cells. The plain Topps #423 base card traded four times in PSA 10 between $17 and $39.
