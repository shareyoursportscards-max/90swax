1993 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1993 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

1993 Ken Griffey Jr. card prices range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $42,000 for the 1993 Finest Refractor #110 in PSA 10.

Has any 1993 Ken Griffey Jr. card price moved recently?

Yes — the 1993 Topps Base #179 in PSA 9 most recently sold for $65 on November 18, 2026 via auction, up 225% from $20 before that.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1993 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

A raw copy of the 1993 Finest Refractor #110 has sold for $8,256 (September 20, 2026 via auction).
