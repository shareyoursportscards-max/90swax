1994 Griffey Card Value FAQ

How much is a 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card prices range from a few dollars for common raw base cards up to $3,700 for the 1994 Finest Refractor #232 in PSA 9.

Has any 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card price moved recently?

Yes — the 1994 Leaf Slideshow #9 in PSA 8 most recently sold for $45 on October 29, 2026, Buy It Now, up 181% from $16 before that.

What is a raw (ungraded) 1994 Ken Griffey Jr. card worth?

A raw copy of the 1994 Finest Refractor #232 has sold for $1,795 (November 15, 2026, Buy It Now).
