5 Ken Griffey Jr. Cards Trending Up and 5 Trending Down (November 2026)

November 22, 2026 · 4 min read · real eBay sold prices

A PSA 9 1996 Metal Universe Griffey sold for $888.88 on November 12. In October one sold for $530. That's the biggest gainer I'm tracking right now, and the biggest loser comes out of the same set.

Here are five Ken Griffey Jr. cards trending up and five trending down. For each one I'm comparing the latest sale to an earlier sale of the same card in the same grade, and I'm listing the sales in between too, because two sales don't always tell the whole story.

How I measured this: every price is an eBay sale I logged. The percent is the latest sale compared to the earlier sale I name.

Trending up

1990 Score #560 — PSA 10

Sold Buy It Now for $185 on November 14. That's up 9% from $170 on October 3.

Another second-year Griffey. In between, one sold for $108 on October 31, so this card dipped hard before it came back.

1992 Upper Deck #424 — PSA 10

Sold Buy It Now for $130 on November 16. That's up 14% from $114 on September 30.

PSA 10 population of 563, and there's a pretty cool image on the back. The 14% is the clean number, but it's been a ride in between: $151 on October 6, $240 on October 11, $200 on October 28, $132 on November 4 and $128 on November 15.

→ 1992 Upper Deck prices

1990 Topps Big Baseball #250 — PSA 10

Sold at auction for $408 on November 13 after 28 bids. That's up 24% from a $330 Buy It Now sale on October 8.

Another second-year card, with a PSA 10 population of 217. No other sales in between.

1992 Score All-Star #436 — PSA 10

Sold for $299.99 on November 17. That's up 33% from a $225 Buy It Now on October 7, in about a month.

This is the All-Star card in the 1992 Score set, with a PSA 10 population of 139. One sale in between went for $391 on November 3.

→ 1992 Score prices

1996 Metal Universe #107 — PSA 9

Sold Buy It Now for $888.88 on November 12. That's up 68% from a $530 auction on October 11.

These are extremely difficult to grade. The back edges on Metal Universe are prone to chipping and edge wear, and the edges and corners on this copy look really clean. PSA 9 population of 158. In between, one sold for $661 on November 2.

→ 1996 Metal Universe prices

Trending down

1990 Upper Deck Mariners Checklist #24 — PSA 10

Sold Best Offer for $120 on November 10. That's down 14% from a $140 Buy It Now on November 1.

Since then it's gone lower. One sold for $89 on November 17.

→ 1990 Upper Deck prices

1990 Donruss #365 — PSA 10

Sold at auction for $208.50 on November 10 after 11 bids. That's down 30% from a $300 Buy It Now on September 28.

Another second-year Griffey. Since then one sold for $188 on November 15.

→ 1990 Donruss prices

1991 Topps #790 — PSA 10

Sold at auction for $140.50 on November 10 after 24 bids. That's down 43% from $247 on October 13.

Base card, PSA 10 population of 492. The sales in between were $206 on October 31, $202 on November 3 and $200 on November 6, so it sat around $200 for a week and then dropped about $60.

→ 1991 Topps prices

1990 Upper Deck #156 — PSA 10

Sold at auction for $127.50 on November 19 after 17 bids. That's down 48% from $243 on October 13.

This card has a huge PSA 10 population of 2,681, and it sells a lot. I logged 16 sales between those two dates, and 14 of them landed between $163 and $209. The other two were $138 on November 11 and $250 on November 12.

→ 1990 Upper Deck prices

1996 Metal Universe Titanium #3 — PSA 9

Sold at auction for $304 on November 18 after 17 bids. That's down 53% from a $650 Buy It Now on October 11.

This one's odd, because it's from the same Metal Universe set as the biggest gainer. The Titanium is an insert in that set. No other sales in between.

→ 1996 Metal Universe prices

What the numbers say

  • The five gainers went up between 9% and 68%. The five losers went down between 14% and 53%.
  • Eight of the ten cards are from 1990 to 1992. The other two are both 1996 Metal Universe, the biggest gainer and the biggest loser.
  • Three of the gainers had a sale in between that went the other way or past the final price: the 1990 Score dipped to $108, the 1992 Upper Deck hit $240, and the 1992 Score All-Star hit $391.
  • On the most-traded card here, the 1990 Upper Deck #156, 14 of the 16 sales in between stayed between $163 and $209 before the $127.50 sale.
